We're starting a blog to share deeper context on major Arcane updates, upcoming deprecations, and migrations.

Release summaries and commit logs are great for seeing what shipped in each version, but they don't always leave room to explain why a change was made or provide walk-throughs for breaking migrations. This space gives us room for longer-form notes that need more detail.

## What we'll cover

- **Deprecations** — Advance notice on configuration flags, environment variables, or workflows slated for removal, along with timelines and replacement options.
- **Migrations** — Practical guides and step-by-step instructions for navigating major releases and architectural updates.
- **Notable updates** — Walk-throughs of major features, tooling improvements, and new capabilities across Arcane.

If you prefer reading via a feed reader, you can subscribe using our <Link href="/rss.xml" data-sveltekit-reload>RSS feed</Link>. For critical notices, we'll also pin an announcement banner across the documentation.
