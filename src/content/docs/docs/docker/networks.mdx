## Browse networks

Open **Networks** in the sidebar. The table shows each network's name, driver, subnet, and gateway.

## Create a network

1. Click **Create Network** and enter a name.
2. Optional: pick a driver (`bridge`, `overlay`, etc.) and set subnet, gateway, and advanced options. **IP Range** limits container addresses to a CIDR range, such as `172.20.0.128/25` within a `172.20.0.0/24` subnet.
3. Click **Create**.

## Inspect a network

Click a network's name to see its ID, driver, subnet, gateway, and attached containers with their assigned IPv4 and IPv6 addresses.

## Remove a network

1. Open the row's dropdown and click the trash icon.
2. Confirm.

> [!NOTE]
> Networks used by containers can't be removed, and Docker's defaults (`bridge`, `host`, `none`) can never be removed.

## Ports view

Open **Ports** from the Networks area to see which ports are published to the host, which containers expose ports without publishing them, and which `host:port` combinations are taken. Search, sort, and page across the whole environment.
