## Create a network

1. Open **Networks** and click **Create Network**.
2. Enter a name.
3. Optional: pick a driver (`bridge`, `overlay`, etc.) and set subnet, gateway, and advanced options. **IP Range** limits container addresses to a CIDR range, such as `172.20.0.128/25` within a `172.20.0.0/24` subnet.
4. Click **Create**.

## Browse networks

Open **Networks** in the sidebar. The table shows each network's name, driver, subnet, and gateway.

## Inspect a network

Click a network's name. You see its ID, driver, subnet, gateway, and attached containers with their IPv4 and IPv6 addresses.

## Remove a network

1. Open the row's dropdown and click the trash icon.
2. Confirm.

Networks used by containers can't be removed. Docker's defaults (`bridge`, `host`, `none`) can never be removed.

## Ports view

Open **Ports** from the Networks area. It shows:

- Ports published to the host.
- Containers that expose ports without publishing them.
- Which `host:port` combinations are taken.

Search, sort, and page across the whole environment.
